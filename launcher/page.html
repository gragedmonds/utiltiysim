UTILITY STUDIO

Your simulations, on this computer.

Everything runs and stays here: the town, the year, the results. Nothing is uploaded. The engine downloads once and is kept in your storage folder.

Storage folder…

A local or mapped drive, such as P:\UtilitySim. Choosing another folder opens another library and leaves this one where it is.

Connecting…

Open Utility Studio in your browser →

Keep this window open while you use Utility Studio. To share a simulation, copy its code in Studio and paste it into Utility Studio on another computer.
