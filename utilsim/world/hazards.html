A risk increase of 0.05 adds 5% of the base hazard for each year of age. Ages are explicit scenario assumptions for each utility's infrastructure cohort. Time advances these ages; repairing a fault or replacing a meter does not reset them. Editing an age records a new assumption. Untick the option to return to existing flat failure rates.

Set baseline failure rates in electricity/gas faults, water leaks and sewer laterals. A zero baseline remains zero. This screen does not enable those models automatically.
