When a delivered notice prompts a request for help

A simulated household or business may request payment help only after learning of an actually delivered notice for an already known, due and unpaid invoice, with insufficient available funds. This is an opt-in customer behavior scenario.

Reaction assumptions

First-noticing probability is evaluated on eligible physical UTC days until the episode is noticed. Zero prevents first noticing; one notices on the first eligible day. Already-noticed episodes can still repeat at zero. Turn off Generate payment-help contacts to pause all new contacts. Delivery delay is 0–2,592,000 seconds (30 days). A contact appears in the recipient feed only after its availability time. Repeats require a continuing eligible condition and stop at the configured limit.

Pending payment reservations and the essential payment reserve reduce available cash. A notice cannot create an invoice or change the known amount. Missing or undelivered documents are not customer knowledge. Cashflow, payment intentions and physical service-problem contacts keep their own controls.

Administrator truth · cancel local cruise before edits · managed runs retain their clock ownership. Pausing stops new generation while preserving recorded contacts. This screen cannot inject invoices/notices, settle payments, grant repayment plans or resolve enterprise cases.
