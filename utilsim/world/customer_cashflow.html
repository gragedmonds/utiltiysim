Essential expenses

Income is credited before expenses when both occur on the same day. A 14-day interval means exactly fourteen UTC physical days; there are no payroll holiday, month-end or daylight-saving adjustments. Zero disables a stream.

Unfunded expenses are recorded for that occurrence only. They do not create debt, invoices or future catch-up charges. Income that would exceed the $10,000,000,000.00 cash limit is skipped in full and recorded. Pausing does not erase history or accrue missed payments. Occupancy changes block the old cohort's cashflow.

Cancel local cruise before editing; managed runs keep their clock ownership. Changes affect only future unprocessed days. Preserve an existing first date to keep its cadence, or choose a new first date on or after the next physical day.
