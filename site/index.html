RUNS ON YOUR COMPUTER

A place. A utility. A year to explore.

Utility Studio builds a seeded town, its electric, gas and water networks and its customers, then runs a year of meter-to-cash operations with the scenarios you inflict. It all happens on your own computer, offline, and every result stays there.

Download and open

One small file. The engine downloads once on first start, into a folder you choose.

Set up your simulation

Environment, utility, staffing and how the year begins. Tweak any setting before you lock it in.

3

Share it as a code

Copy a simulation code like UTS1-NDBCM-0ZQQ9… and paste it into Utility Studio on another computer: same town, same seed, same scenarios.
